# FBF app and YARBRO Physique Pro app — official explainer videos (Nov 2026)

Source: Bryan's official explainer videos, each in a 16:9 full version and a 9:16 short version.
Spelling notes: the coaching app is branded **YARBRO Physique Pro**, built by coach **Phil Yarborough**.
The FBF app is "Forged by Freedom, powered by YARBRO". Coach Bryan is the voice of the FBF AI Coach.
The library figures quoted in the FBF video (290,000+ sources, 836 million words) were current when it was made.
The library has grown since then.

## Forged by Freedom app (app.forgedbyfreedom.org)

Tagline: "Your coaching. One app." Ends with "Forged by Freedom, powered by YARBRO. Monsters are real."

- **Today, "Your plan for today":** one screen each day holding:
  - the coach-approved workout
  - meals and macros
  - supplements
  - water
  - quick links to message Coach Phil and to send photos, weight and how the week went
- **Train, "The week your coach built":** pick the day, and every exercise, set and rep range is ready to log.
- **Meals, "Say what you ate":** tap the mic, say what you ate off plan, and the app logs it against calories and macros.
- **Meal plan, "Built to your exact macros":** built day by day to exact targets, and every meal is one tap to log.
- **Shopping list, "Sorted by aisle. Delivered.":** the week's meal plan becomes a shopping list sorted by aisle, with delivery from Instacart, Walmart or Amazon Fresh.
- **Protocol, "Every item. Every day.":** supplements and protocols are scheduled, and adherence is tracked daily (screen: Cycle, Supplements & Support).
- **FBF AI Coach, "290,000+ expert sources":**
  - ask anything about training, nutrition, peptides or protocols
  - real answers in Coach Bryan's voice ("Ask Coach Bryan"); tap Listen to hear the answer out loud
- **Bloodwork, "Every marker. Flagged.":** upload a lab report (PDF or photo) or paste results; it pulls out every marker and flags anything out of range, and keeps past reports.
- **Daily check-in, "Two minutes a day":** vitals, mood, stress, sleep and how the day went.
- **Weekly check-in, "Show your work":** a fasted weigh-in, a 1–10 rating of the week (diet, training, sleep quality, energy and so on) and three photos, sent straight to the coach.
- **Progress, "Every change has a story":** photo comparisons, weight trend, streaks and daily targets.
- **Body scan, "360° body composition":** a video turn, tape measurements at 7 sites and a 7-site caliper test. No BMI; body fat is measured two ways.
- **Posing studio, "Dial in your lines":**
  - mandatory poses for every division (Men's Physique, Classic Physique, Bodybuilding, Women's Bikini, Wellness, Figure)
  - film a pose, send it to the coach, and get written corrections back
- **Peptide research, "Know what you're taking":** a library explaining what each peptide does and what to expect before starting (for example CJC-1295 with DAC, BPC-157, tesamorelin, thymosin alpha-1, retatrutide, tesofensine).
- **Coach console, "Coach in plain English":**
  - the coach types or says the change in plain English, previews it, confirms, and the client is notified instantly
  - also holds a posing review queue
  - alerts flag off-plan days (last 14 days) before they become a problem
- **Get started:** app.forgedbyfreedom.org

## YARBRO Physique Pro (Phil Yarborough's coaching app)

Tagline: "Built for the work. Designed for progress." Short version: "Coaching built for the stage."

- **Your athlete profile, "A private coaching space":** every athlete gets a private space with their profile front and center.
- **Coming up, "Always counting down":** competition target date, next check-in deadline and a countdown to each.
- **Your coach, "One tap to Phil":** message Coach Phil directly from the daily plan.
- **Daily weigh-in, "The bigger picture":** the 7-day average, week-over-week change and yesterday at a glance.
- **Calendar:** a shared calendar keeps athlete and coach on one schedule, with alerts as dates approach.
- **Training studio, "Train with focus":**
  - published program, cardio quick logs, workout plans, progression and history
  - **Movement library, "216 exercises":** 13 focused collections, searchable by muscle and equipment
- **Nutrition, "Every drink, accounted for":** water tracked against the coach's target, alongside the prescribed meals and food log.
- **Messages, "Photos, progress, conversations":** in one thread, with an automatic update whenever the coach changes the schedule or nutrition.
- **Check-ins, "Every week, on record":** weekly photos and answers go to the coach, with a full history of every submission and its review status.
- **Progress:** weight trajectory over 7 days, 30 days or all time.
- **Protocols & supplements, "Clearly recorded":** amounts, schedules and dated cycles, with full version history.
- **Labs, "Bloodwork, verified":** stores bloodwork reports, flags anything out of range and tracks trends over time. Results are verified by the coach.
- **Posing studio:** upload a clip and get corrections approved by the coach.
- **Show day, "Ready for the stage":** a dedicated show-day workspace with confirmed times, coach updates, posing cues and backstage photos, refreshing live.
- **Install, "iPhone · Android · desktop":**
  - installs from the browser
  - iPhone/iPad: open the invitation in Safari, sign in, then Share → Add to Home Screen
  - Android: open in Chrome, sign in, then Install app or Add to Home screen
